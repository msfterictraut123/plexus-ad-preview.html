How visibility works

Header: directly under the site navigation. In-feed: placed between alert cards. Alert discussion: an incoming-style sponsored message within a specific alert conversation.

Click-through

Each Plexus unit links to the designated Plexus Energy page with placement-specific UTM content: header_1, feed_3, or chat_1.

Ad fallback

An approved paid campaign in the same placement takes priority. In-feed keeps Google AdSense available while the Plexus image loads or if the Plexus asset fails.

GA4 measurement

OutageKE sends GA4 promotion events when a custom ad becomes visible and when it is clicked. Compare select_promotion clicks against view_promotion impressions by promotion ID/name to calculate CTR.

Events: view_promotion / select_promotion
Parameters: promotion_id, promotion_name, creative_name, creative_slot
